Manba: Instagram post (@codingknowledge, 2026-11-23). Har bir repo tekshirildi.

## O‘rnatildi

**SuperClaude Framework** (SuperClaude-Org/SuperClaude_Framework, MIT, `fe68862`):
- `.claude/commands/sc/` — 30 ta buyruq: `/sc:troubleshoot`, `/sc:analyze`, `/sc:implement`, `/sc:test`, `/sc:brainstorm`, `/sc:research`, `/sc:estimate`, `/sc:document` va boshqalar.
- `.claude/agents/` — 20 ta mutaxassis agent: `root-cause-analyst`, `security-engineer`, `frontend-architect`, `quality-engineer`, `technical-writer` va boshqalar.
- Litsenziya: `.claude/LICENSE-SuperClaude`.
- Ataylab olinmadi: hook'lar (har sessiyada skript va har tahrirdan keyin qo‘shimcha tekshiruv, ishni sekinlashtiradi), MCP sozlamalari va Python o‘rnatuvchisi.
